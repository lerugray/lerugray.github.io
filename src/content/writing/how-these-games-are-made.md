I design board wargames for Conflict Simulations LLC, and I make my video games the same way: on paper first.

**What I do.** The premise, the rules and systems, the writing and the history, the difficulty and tuning, and what stays or gets cut. I play the builds and send them back until they do what the design says.

**What AI does.** It writes the code. I use Claude as my programmer, working from my design documents and rulings. Art and sound vary from game to game: some of the art is drawn by that code to my direction, and some of the music I recorded myself. Each game's itch page carries its own AI disclosure, and on every one of them it says yes.

**Why.** I spent about twenty years trying to learn to code well enough to build the games in my notebooks, and implementation was always the wall. The longer version is in [Out of the Notebook](/writing/out-of-the-notebook).

If that's a dealbreaker for you, I understand, and no hard feelings. If you play one, I'd like to hear what you think of the design.
