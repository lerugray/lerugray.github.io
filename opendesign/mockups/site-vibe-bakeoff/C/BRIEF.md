# Direction C — "Editorial portfolio, case-study led"  (read ../BRIEF-COMMON.md first; it binds)

A clean, light editorial portfolio, the way a top independent designer or a small studio presents client work:
white or warm-white paper, a strong editorial serif for headlines (Fraunces, Newsreader or Instrument Serif), a
neutral sans for UI (Inter Tight or IBM Plex Sans), a disciplined 12-column grid, large images, lots of air.
The organizing idea is CASE STUDIES:
- Present each commission as a case-study card or spread: Napoleon at Bay, Flattop, Paths of Glory, For the People
  (each: its screenshot, its caption verbatim, and its pricing class from the Pricing tiers where CONTENT.md names
  it: Napoleon at Bay = the $600 class; Flattop, Paths of Glory, For the People = the $800 class). Do not invent any
  other per-game facts.
- Bloody April as the featured client case: the client paragraph, both Bloody April screenshots, and the full
  Greenwood testimonial with a verbatim pull-quote.
- The World Undone as "try one" with both links prominent.
- First screen: title, dek, a large real screenshot, price range, email action.
- Home: name, dek and bio set like a magazine opener, with the four real offers from Work-with-me as a clear,
  scannable index.
- Do NOT reuse the parchment/oxblood/atlas look or its plate labels. Calm, confident, expensive; color comes from the
  screenshots plus at most one accent.
