# Direction B — "Dark studio"  (read ../BRIEF-COMMON.md first; it binds)

An ambitious, dark product-studio look: the site of someone who ships software products. Think of how a
high-end independent studio presents its games and tools: near-black or very deep ink background, the module
screenshots glowing as the brightest things on the page (they are rich, colorful map art; let them carry the color),
big confident display type, a monospace accent for data (prices, stats, rule citations), crisp thin rules.
- First screen: a bold headline (the page title and dek, verbatim), a large screenshot treated as a product hero
  (e.g. Napoleon at Bay or Bloody April, maybe slightly angled or layered with a second shot), price-from and an
  email button.
- A stat band from the real numbers only (7 commissions, 4 clients, since July 2026, 600 automated rules checks,
  previews within 2 days).
- The screenshots as a gallery with captions that reads like a product tour.
- The testimonial large and luminous; pricing as four crisp tier cards.
- Do NOT reuse the parchment/oxblood/atlas look or its plate labels. Do not go neon-cyberpunk or "AI startup"
  purple gradient either; aim for refined and expensive. Suggested type: Space Grotesk or Inter Tight for display/UI,
  JetBrains Mono or IBM Plex Mono for data; your call.
