Meta facts (may be shown as a strip/badge row): Digital modules · Commissions open, selective · $400 to monster · Brooklyn, NY

Intro:
I build digital modules of print wargames on commission. The map, the counters, and the procedures are translated
into a local engine. You unzip a folder and double-click index.html. It runs in your browser. No installs, no
accounts, no internet connection required. If you have no one to play, the module can play the other side.

### What a module includes
Automated bookkeeping and chart lookups. Hotseat play with information masking for cards and hidden movement. Every
roll logs the cited rule. Games save to local files. The price includes a computer opponent that can take either side.
